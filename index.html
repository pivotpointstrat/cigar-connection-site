The humidor

A walk-in humidor, kept properly

The centre of the room is the humidor — a cedar-lined, glass-fronted room lined with boxes, kept at the temperature and humidity a cigar is meant to be kept at. What you light is what the maker intended.

Come in and browse. Take your time. Whether you've smoked for thirty years or this is your first, we'll help you find something you'll enjoy — and tell you honestly what's worth the money.

Why come up

Three good reasons to climb the stairs

A real humidor

Not a cabinet behind the bar — a walk-in room. Browse the shelves, and ask us anything about what you're looking at.

A room built to linger in

Brick, walnut panelling, leather armchairs and low, warm light. No rush and no queue — just a comfortable chair and good company.

Yours for the evening

Take the whole lounge for a private group. A birthday, a deal closed, or simply a table of people who would rather not be overheard.

The room

Brick, leather, and a wall of pictures

Exposed brick, dark walnut panelling, and the framed cigar art and portraits that line the walls. On one side, a lit bar; at the back, the humidor glowing.

It's the kind of room that makes an hour feel like two — in the best way.

The selection

Our range draws on the classic producing regions — the Caribbean, the Dominican Republic and Nicaragua — and we keep it to the names worth knowing rather than filling the shelves with everything.

Among the houses on our shelves:

  • Cohiba
  • Romeo y Julieta
  • Joya
  • Perdomo
  • VegaFina
  • White Label
  • AVO

New arrivals land regularly — the shelves are worth a look in person.
