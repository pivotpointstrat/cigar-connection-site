The upper level can be reserved for private groups — a birthday, a celebration, a deal closed properly, or simply an evening where the only people listening are the ones you invited.

What you get

The lounge, your way

Your group gets the lounge: the leather chairs, the tables, the wall of framed cigar art, and the humidor at your back. We'll look after the drinks, and arrange food up from the Paparazzi kitchen downstairs.

If you'd like, we'll talk your guests through the cigars — an easy, enjoyable way to open an evening for a group where not everyone is a smoker.
