Finding us

On the Riverside

We're on Preah Sisowath Quay, the riverside road in Daun Penh — the stretch along the Tonle Sap where the riverfront walk is.

Any tuk-tuk or taxi driver in Phnom Penh will know Paparazzi Bar & Kitchen. If you'd rather navigate yourself, the Maps button above drops you straight on the door.

Coming by car? Ask us about parking when you call.

Before you come

Anything you'd like to know

Do we need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome — but for a group, or a weekend evening, it's worth calling ahead so we can keep chairs for you.

Bringing non-smokers?

More than welcome. There's food from the kitchen downstairs, a full bar, and comfortable chairs — plenty of guests come for the company and the room.

What should we wear?

Nothing formal. Come as you are — smart casual is the natural fit for the room.
