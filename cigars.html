How to choose

If you're new to cigars

Say so — that's the easiest conversation we have. We'll start you on something mild and smooth, and we won't make it complicated.

If you know what you like

Tell us the last one you enjoyed and we'll find you something in the same country of thought — or push you one step on, if you'd like.

Paired with a drink

Ask us what to drink with it. A rum, a whisky or a draught Guinness will all do different and excellent things to a cigar.
